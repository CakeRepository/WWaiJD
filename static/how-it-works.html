How it Works

Learn how we retrieve and ground responses directly in Scripture.

The Mission

To help people explore the Bible and find compassionate, scripture-grounded answers using modern AI.

The Technology

We use a technique called RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation). When you ask a question, our system searches through the entire Holy Bible for verses relevant to your situation, then uses AI to summarize and present those exact passages with direct context.

Bible Versions

We support 9+ Bible translations including KJV, ESV, NIV, NASB, NKJV, NLT, CSB, ASV, and WEB.

You can switch between versions using the dropdown selector, and even compare the same verse across all translations.

Core Features

Ask Questions: Get scripture-based answers with multiple response styles (Balanced, Comfort, Clarity, Challenge, Blessing).

Read the Bible: Full book and chapter reader with verse comparisons.

Daily Devotional: Inspired daily reflections with study streak tracking.

Community Prayer Wall: Share and pray for anonymous community requests.

Keyboard Shortcuts

Ctrl/Cmd + K: Focus the search box

1-5: Switch response modes

B: Open Bible reader
